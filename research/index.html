Research

AI + Optimization + Physics for Resilient Energy Grids

At P²-LAB we work where power systems, optimization and machine learning meet. Our aim is thoughtful AI: physics-inspired methods that keep grid operation and planning secure as networks turn decentralised and renewable-heavy.

Areas: structured-data FMs / AI for power systems / learning for optimization / power systems & other

Structured-Data Foundation Models

4 papers

Prior-data fitted networks that learn in context. With Decoupled-Value Attention, one pretrained model solves power flow on network configurations it has never seen — no retraining, no gradient updates — and the same idea carries to tabular science beyond the grid.

Supported by ANRF PMECRG 2025–2028.

AI for Power Systems

18 papers

Gaussian-process surrogates that turn power flow into a closed-form, differentiable map, V = f(s). From it come probabilistic voltage envelopes, contingency screening in seconds and guarantees on voltage risk — without Monte Carlo.

Selected papers
  • Learning Power Flow with Confidence: A Probabilistic Guarantee Framework for Voltage Risk Electric Power Systems Research, 2027
  • Gaussian Processes in Power Systems: Techniques, Applications, and Future Works Applied Energy, 2026
  • A Framework for Analytical Power Flow Solution Using Gaussian Process Learning IEEE Trans. Sustainable Energy, 2022
